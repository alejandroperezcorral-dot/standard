What does this tool do?

This tool helps you negotiate and close fashion purchases by automatically calculating whether the supplier's price meets your margin target. Instead of doing manual Excel calculations, you enter the article data and the tool instantly tells you whether the price works.

1. Enter the article
Fill in the product details: FOB, RRP, origin, weight, category...
2. The tool calculates
Automatically calculates the total landed cost (LDP) and margin (IMU) using real freight and customs rates.
3. Negotiate with confidence
See the maximum FOB you can pay and how much you need to negotiate down to hit your margin target.

Form fields Ã¢â‚¬â€ explained one by one

Style (Modelo)
Article reference code. E.g. BJN001. Unique identifier in your system.
Description
Article name. E.g. "Slim Fit Denim Jeans 420GSM". Appears on PDF product sheets.
Supplier
The supplier manufacturing the article. Configurable in Settings. Sets the default origin.
Origin
Country of manufacture. E.g. Bangladesh, Vietnam, China. Affects freight cost and customs duties.
Transport
Shipping mode: SEA-TRUCK (ship + truck), SEA-TRAIN (ship + rail), AIR (air freight). Each mode has a different cost and transit time configured in Settings Ã¢â€ â€™ Freight.
Category
Product category: Jeans, T-Shirts, Outerwear... Determines the customs rate and units per container.
Department
Business department. E.g. DENIM, KNIT_TOPS. Used for filtering and grouping in reports.
Weight (kg)
Net garment weight in kg. Very important: affects the fixed duty (Ã¢â€šÂ¬/kg) and the units-per-container calculation.
Units
Units to buy. More units = more full containers = cheaper freight cost per unit.
PVP (RRP) Ã¢â€šÂ½
Retail selling price in Russian rubles. The basis for IMU margin calculation. Converted to USD after deducting VAT (20% by default).
FOB R1 / R2 / R3
Supplier-quoted prices in each negotiation round. R1 is the opening offer, R3 the last before close. Each change recalculates IMU in real time.
FOB Closed
Final agreed price with the supplier. When you fill this field and set status to CLOSED, the article moves to the Closed tab and this price is used for all final calculations.
FSD (First Sale Date)
Planned first sale date. The tool automatically calculates HOD (Hand-Off Date) by subtracting the transit days for the chosen transport mode.
HOD (Hand-Off Date)
Latest factory departure date. Calculated automatically: HOD = FSD Ã¢Ë†â€™ transit_days. This is the date you give the supplier to ensure goods arrive on time.
ST% (Sell-Through)
Estimated sell-through percentage of units purchased. Default 65%. Used in replenishment planning.
Target IMU
Margin target for this specific article. Default 72%. The colour indicator and FOB Target are both calculated against this value.
Tax % (IVA)
Applicable VAT percentage. Default 20% (Russia). Change it in the header if operating in another market. Affects the net RRP and IMU calculation.
USD/RUB, EUR/USD, USD/AED
Exchange rates configurable in the header. They update all calculations in real time. Refresh them whenever rates change.

Step by step Ã¢â‚¬â€ How to use the tool

1
Select supplier and origin
In the left sidebar, choose the Supplier and Origin. The tool automatically filters the available transport modes for that route.
2
Enter RRP and FOB R1
As soon as you enter the RRP (retail price in rubles) and FOB R1 (supplier's opening offer), the preview box shows IMU, LDP and FOB Target calculated instantly.
3
Negotiate with the supplier
If IMU is red, the "FOB Target" tells you what price you need to reach. Save R2 and R3 as negotiation progresses to keep a history.
4
Close the article
When the price is acceptable, fill in FOB Closed with the agreed price and set the status to CLOSED. The article moves automatically to the Closed tab.
5
Generate PDF product sheets
In the Closed tab, select articles using the checkboxes and click Product Sheets PDF. A product sheet is generated per article with photo, prices and logistics data.
6
Export to Excel
The Ã¢â€ â€œ XLS button in Closed exports all closed articles to an Excel file with all fields and calculated metrics.
